# Raw data: source link

The raw WHO files are not stored in this repository. Download them from the source below.

- **Source:** WHO Global Health Observatory (GHO): <https://www.who.int/data/gho>
- **Theme:** Service coverage index and components (SDG 3.8.1)
- **Download date:** 2026-01-06
- **Indicators used:** `UHC_INDEX_REPORTED`, `UHC_SCI_RMNCH`, `UHC_SCI_INFECT`, `UHC_SCI_NCD`, `UHC_SCI_CAPACITY`, `UHC_AVAILABILITY_SCORE`

The merged, cleaned country-year table (4,680 rows) is in the **Raw Data** tab of `sheets/healthcare-facility-performance-tracker.xlsx`. Every cleaning step is recorded in its **Cleaning Log** tab.
